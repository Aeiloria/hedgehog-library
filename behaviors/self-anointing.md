# Self-Anointing / Frothing

**Status:** candidate  
**Also called:** frothing, pretect-contortion anointing, scent ritual  

## What it is

The hedgehog coats itself with a sensory medium (foam, oil, resin, plant juices, soil) while in a high-engagement, low-vigilance bliss state. Not a high — a **ritual of safety, scent, and self**.

## Caduceus zones that trigger it

| Zone | Medium | Character |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Bed 3 Frothing | Mint + foam / floral oils | Explosive, mint-flecked, foam volume high |
| Layer 2 / Bed 4 | Pine resin, softwood ash | Sticky mineral memory, slower set |
| Base notes | Cedar + patchouli | Home & comfort anchor froth |
