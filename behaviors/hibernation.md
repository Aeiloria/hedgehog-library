## Core idea

Hibernation is **controlled torpor**, not normal sleep. Metabolic activity almost stands still to survive cold when insect prey vanishes.

## Preparation (Autumn)

- **White fat** — primary fuel for winter
- **Brown fat** — around shoulders/spine; rapid heat for arousal
- **Hibernaculum** — nest of dry leaves, twigs, grass, moss (log piles, under sheds, dense brush, compost heaps)

## Torpor physiology (ambient ~<10°C)

| Function | Active | Torpor |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Body temp | ~35°C | ~1–4°C |
| Heart rate | 190–280 bpm | 20–50 bpm |
| Respiration | 25–50 /min | ~1 breath every few minutes |

## Arousals

Periodic partial warm-ups use brown fat; costly — underweight hedgehogs may not survive repeated arousals.
