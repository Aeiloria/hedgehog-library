## Essence

The Disney “Hakuna Matata” mossy-log bug reveal is, to a hedgehog, not comedy — it is **Hedgehog Valhalla**: dark cover, damp rot, high-protein chaos, then thigmotaxis food coma under the log.

> Snout-plow. Crunch. Collapse. No sharing table.

This node designs the **research / sanctuary version** of that log — not a cage, not a spectacle, a feeding habitat that ends in safe rest.

---

## Biological harvest pattern (design constraints)

1. **Snout-shovel, not suction** — wedge under bark, plow rot, snort to clear dirt  
2. **Rapid-fire crunch** — high-frequency bites; lawnmower pace on grubs/worms/beetles  
3. **Thigmotaxis during feast** — heavy cool moss/wood on the back is part of the reward  
4. **Food coma in place** — after harvest they prefer to shimmy deeper and sleep under cover  

**Do not** design feeders that require open-field vulnerability or social sharing. Hedgehogs are solitary around food.

## Goldilocks decay (ideal log station)

| Parameter | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Log | fallen oak (or similar), moss roof |
| Condition | late soft rot: roof holds, under-layer spongy |
| Climate | deep shade, high humidity, fungal musk |
| Entry | gap between wood and soil for snout wedge |
| Aftercare | leave excavated pocket intact as mini-den |

Caduceus practical version: **ash-dusted hugelkultur feeder** with fat beetle larvae + chamomile-infused leaf mold under a willow-shade canopy.
