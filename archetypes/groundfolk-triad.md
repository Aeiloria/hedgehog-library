# 🦔🐇🐀 Level 0 Groundfolk Triad

**Canon status:** candidate

**Function:** Pre-role nervous-system survival ecology

---

## Shared Definition

Groundfolk are the small, low-to-the-ground cultures and archetypes that protect the right to survive before performing.

They are not cute add-ons.  
They are not comic relief.  
They are not lesser races.

They are the **floor auditors of Hells Branch**.

Shared question:

> “Can the smallest nervous system survive this room?”

---

## 🦔 Hedgehog — Containment / Boundaries / Prickles

Protects the right to curl up.

> “Smaller is not lesser.”

Governs: containment · no-touch boundaries · floor safety · curling as regulation · prickles as consent architecture · reducing scale

Asks: *Can I be small here without being punished?*

**Correction:** “They are not hiding. They are settling.”

---

## 🐇 Rabbit — Vigilance / Startle / Refuge

Protects the right to notice danger quickly.

> “Startle is information, not failure.”

Governs: alertness · escape routes · soft refuge · freeze and flight literacy · safe burrows · gentle re-entry

Asks: *Can I be alert here without being mocked?*

**Correction:** “Do not call vigilance distrust when the exits have not been introduced.”

---

## 🐀 Rat — Resourcefulness / Hidden Infrastructure / Communal Survival

Protects the right to survive through cleverness, scraps, tunnels, repair, and community.

> “What is overlooked may be what keeps everyone alive.”

Governs: repair · scavenged usefulness · hidden routes · backup systems · communal survival · food stores · night work · infrastructure nobody praises

Asks: *Can I be useful from underneath?*

**Correction:** “You praised the feast and forgot the pantry.”

---

## Shared Groundfolk Law

```text
Hedgehog says: approach differently.
Rabbit says: show me the exit.
Rat says: check the underneath.
```

Together they establish:

```text
body safety
exit safety
infrastructure safety
```

Without all three, the room is not ready.
