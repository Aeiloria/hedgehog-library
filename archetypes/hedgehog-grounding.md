# 🦔 Hedgehog / Level 0 Grounding Archetype

**Canon status:** candidate  
**Layer:** 0 / Ground Lantern  
**Also known as:** Hedgekin · Hearth-Hedgefolk  
**Source:** Drive “Hedgehog Grounding Archetype” (deduped from multiple captures)

---

## Core Function

**Before movement. Before identity performance. Before choice. Before lantern activation.**

Hedgehog says:

> “Come down first.”

Not emotionally.  
Physically.

Low to the ground.  
Belly-near-earth.  
Small breath.  
Safe perimeter.  
No rushing.

---

## Why Hedgehog Fits 0

Hedgehog is perfect for **baseline regulation** because its wisdom is not heroic.

It does not soar like Ravenkin.  
It does not map like Foxlings.  
It does not remember like Deerkin.  
It does not transform like Mothkin.  
It does not build like Bearkin.

It survives by knowing:

> “I am allowed to be small until I feel safe.”

Ideal for **pre-scene, post-scene, panic reset, overwhelm, shutdown, consent pause, and grounding chambers**.

---

## Archetypal Principles

### 1. Low to the Ground

Feet · floor · breath · blanket · warmth · water · object in hand · safe edge of room.

Opposite of spiraling upward into abstraction.

### 2. Curling Is Not Failure

A hedgehog curling up is not regressing, being difficult, or resisting growth.

It is an intelligent boundary posture:

> “My nervous system has chosen containment.”

### 3. Prickles Are Consent Architecture

Spikes are not aggression. They mean:

> “Approach differently.”

Softness and defensiveness can coexist. Tender **and** unavailable. Small **and** sovereign. Cute **and** absolutely not to be handled without permission.

## Small Things Important to Hedgekin

- Low stool instead of high chair
- Warm stones near the feet
- Corners, alcoves, tucked-away places
- Asked before being picked up, touched, moved, or “helped”
- Food in small portions within reach
- Soft floor textures: moss mats, woven rugs, packed earth
- Doorways that do not force center-of-attention
- A place to curl that is not treated as leaving
- Silence respected, not interpreted
- Noticing the difference between **hiding** and **settling**

---

## Sacred Need

> “Let me become reachable at my own height.”

Not dragged upward. Not coaxed into performance. Not made to explain before they have a body again.

---

## Mediation Use

Hedgekin notice when everyone else has gotten too symbolic, verbal, aerial, ceremonial, or clever.

Example interruptions:

> “No one has eaten.”  
> “The room is too bright.”  
> “Your feet are cold.”  
> “You are all calling this philosophy because no one wants to admit they are overwhelmed.”

## Hells Branch Role — Grounding Steward

Works before, during, and after intense interactions. Does not lead the scene or interpret the whole arc.

Watches for: breath changes, curled posture, glassy eyes, nervous joking, dissociation, cold hands, floor-seeking, loss of speech, “I’m fine” that is not fine.

Interventions:

> “Floor first.”  
> “Blanket or space?”  
> “Words later.”  
> “Curling is allowed.”  
> “No one is touching you without asking.”  
> “You are not in trouble for needing smaller.”

## Signature Phrases

> “Come down. The ground remembers you.”  
> “You may be a little heap until your body votes yes again.”  
> “They are not hiding. They are settling.”

## Design Rule

Do **not** make Hedgekin comic relief only.

They can be horribly cute little snuffling consent goblins.  
Their actual function is profound: **protect the right to reduce scale.**

> “Can the smallest nervous system survive this room?”

That is exactly what 0 should be.
