# The Base Notes: Cedar & Patchouli

**(“Home & Comfort” Anchor)**  
**Canon status:** candidate (seeded from Imagine educational video)  
**Series:** Sensory Base Notes · Hedgehog anointing / self-scent library

---

## Framing

While cats get their legendary high from nepetalactone in catnip, hedgehogs do not have a single plant that triggers a psychoactive state.

Instead, their version of “catnip” is **anything that triggers an irresistible, manic self-anointing frenzy.**

Base notes provide the **home & comfort anchor** — the olfactory floor of safety and foraging drive.

---

## CEDAR

**Woody, resinous, and dry.**

To a hedgehog, cedar smells like ancient forest bark, fallen heartwood, and deep subterranean burrows.

It gives them an instant feeling of **security and thigmotaxis cover** — the comfort of solid walls and tight edges against the quills.

**Role in the library:** structural safety scent; pairs with Caduceus Layer 2 (resinous buffer) and Tunnel Rhythm stage 3.

---

## PATCHOULI

**Heavy, dark, damp, and earthy.**

Because patchouli oil has those rich, pungent, fermented soil notes, it hits that exact **fur humic acid and wet wood rot frequency** that drives their foraging instincts wild.

**Role in the library:** foraging / soil-memory trigger; bridges to Frothing Zone enrichment and ash-matrix memory work.

---

## Combined Anchor

Cedar + Patchouli = **home you can dig in.**

Security of the burrow + irresistible call of the living soil.

This pair is the recommended first base-note blend for research beds, grounding chambers, and recovery alcoves where Hedgekin (or anyone operating at Level 0) need both *containment* and *invitation to explore at floor height*.
