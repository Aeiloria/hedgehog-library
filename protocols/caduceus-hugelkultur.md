## Structural Shell (Core Canopy) — Multi-Layered Progression

### Layer 1 — Exterior Heavy Timber Canopy
- **Woods:** Kanuka, Kauri, Elm, Oak, and other native hardwoods
- **Function:** Deep, heavy, rot-resistant logs as subterranean fortress walls. Holds moisture for years without collapsing.
- **Role:** Long-term structural integrity and moisture management.

### Layer 2 — Resinous, Aromatic & Gelatinous Buffer Zone
- **Materials:** Manuka, Cedar, Pine, crushed Cacti shoots, Aloe Vera
- **Function:** Antimicrobial oils + cooling, moisture-retaining gel. Protective aromatic buffer.
- **Role:** Defense, cooling, moisture regulation, sensory interest.

### Layer 3 — Comfrey-Glazed / Resin-Glazed Tunnel
- **Materials:** Slow comfrey-drenched clay, tree sap, wood ash, aggregate
- **Function:** Hand-carved, slow-cured tunnel. Comfrey tea supercharges microbial heat. Resin glaze seals while remaining breathable.
- **Role:** Nutrient core, microbial heat engine, safe inner sanctuary.

Typical finished inner tunnel diameter: ~2.4 m at full habitat scale (research beds scale proportionally).

## The Permaculture Herb Matrix (Left-to-Right Sensory Gradient)

Four micro-climates, one continuous sensory gradient.

### Bed 1 — Warm & Savory
- **Plants:** Sage, Lemon Balm, Fennel
- **Profile:** Warm, sweet-herbaceous, anise-like
- **Behavioral effect:** Gentle invitation, steady foraging

### Bed 2 — Floral & Acidic
- **Plants:** Chamomile, Jasmine, Honeysuckle, Tomato, Rosemary
- **Profile:** Bruised tomato leaf musk + indolic florals; “Valhalla” when St. John’s Wort and chamomile are comfrey-soaked
- **Behavioral effect:** Highest recorded belly-flop counts

### Bed 3 — The Frothing / Catnip Zone
- **Plants:** Mugwort, Lavender, Mint
- **Profile:** Dreamlike sharpness, CNS calm, instant full-body pretect-contortion anointing trigger
- **Behavioral effect:** Explosive self-anointing (“frothing”) and high sensory engagement

### Bed 4 — Resin & Cleanse / Restorative
- **Plants:** Rosemary, White Sage, Thyme
- **Profile:** High-terpene cleansing resin
- **Behavioral effect:** Quill maintenance, grounding, preparation for rest

## Weekly Citrus Test Effect

Deliberate sensory fork: light citrus drizzle (lemon or bergamot) on one half of the bed.

- **Non-citrus side** — Deep, earthy, fungal/humic. Quiet foraging, 14-hour deep-core naps.
- **Citrus-infused side** — High-energy, sharp. Nose twitch → half-mast quill visor → manic high-velocity frothing and exploration.

Research + enrichment protocol. Record belly flops, quill twitches, anointing duration, exploration velocity.

## Tunnel Rhythm — Engineered Micro-Climate Sequence

1. **Tight & Hot** (Thermal Sauna / Deep Massage) — Narrow, sun-warmed, heavy dark clay, high pressure.
2. **Wide & Cold** (Cold Plunge / Belly Flop Zone) — Wide mud floor, tuber moisture, ice-cold sphagnum. Zero wall pressure. Satisfied snorts and belly flops.
3. **Resinous & Medium** (Frothing Zone) — Tight cedar & mint, herbal steam, medium pressure. Anointing peak.
4. **Insulated Cave** (14-Hour Sleep Vault) — Fibrous leaf mold, cool, collapse-proof. Full restorative sleep.

Supporting engineering: solar collectors (dark basalt / heavy clay at pinch points); cold siphons (deep clay pipes from swamp-tuber patches).

Typical cycle: ~three hours of blissful hot/cold back-and-forth → total collapse in the sleep vault.
